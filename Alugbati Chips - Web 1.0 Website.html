What Are Alugbati Chips?

Alugbati Chips are a vegetable-based snack inspired by alugbati, a leafy green plant commonly used in Filipino cooking. Alugbati is known for its soft leaves and distinctive green color. By transforming this familiar vegetable into a crispy snack, Alugbati Chips offer a creative alternative to ordinary chips.

Our product aims to introduce people to a different way of enjoying vegetables while promoting creativity, local food resources, and an appreciation for Filipino ingredients.

OUR PRODUCT IDEA: A crispy, flavorful, and locally inspired snack made with alugbati.
